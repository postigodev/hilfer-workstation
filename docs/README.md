# HILFER documentation

**Current baseline:** the engine foundation and application integrations are complete on `main`, through
[PR #7](https://github.com/postigodev/hilfer-workstation/pull/7) and
[PR #8](https://github.com/postigodev/hilfer-workstation/pull/8). The latter was squash-merged as
`f8e7e8a74624f89d5c93aa7cd135fa683e26639c` on November 21, 2026.

On this development branch, J/E/F/G are playable through explicit documentary association
and actual transport observation. Injury playback and personal identity are not required.
Completion of the engine does not mean completion of the game.

## Remaining scope

Real investigation evidence maps, the definitive inference interface, further playable cases, determination
revisions and ending mechanics still require design/approval. Test-only relationships are not canonical content.
The current attachment readiness contract supports presented text; no production attachment viewer or binary
attachment policy has been added. CAM 4 remains an unmapped preview and does not grant CAM-R03 inspection.

Narrative design and any future Investigation & Evidence Architecture document are separate work. Updating
repository documentation does not create or modify Google Drive documents.
