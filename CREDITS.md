# Third-party credits

- **98.css 0.1.21** by Jordan Scales and contributors, MIT. [Upstream package](https://www.npmjs.com/package/98.css/v/0.1.21). HILFER adapts its frames, controls, table headers and status fields, and uses four upstream title bar glyphs. The stylesheet is hosted locally and uses HILFER theme colors. [License](css/vendor/98-css.LICENSE).
- [Arwa's graphics and visual integration credits](assets/arwa/CREDITS.md).

[Regular](https://fontstruct.com/fontstructions/show/1384746) and [bold](https://fontstruct.com/fontstructions/show/1384862) are licensed under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Original notices are in [the font directory](css/vendor/98-css-fonts/).

- **Windows 98 Mail toolbar artwork** by Microsoft. The classroom build uses 16-pixel PNG derivatives of `w98_envelope_open_sheet.ico`, `w98_envelope_closed.ico`, `w98_recycle_bin_full.ico`, and `w98_recycle_bin_file.ico`. [Archive used for extraction](https://github.com/trapd00r/win95-winxp_icons/tree/master/icons). [Alex Meub's catalog](https://alexmeub.com/old-windows-icons/) is the visual reference. These assets are separate from the MIT 98.css glyphs.

The Mail folder tree uses 16 × 16 derivatives from the supplied Windows icon collection in `references/win98-icons/ico/`: `mailbox_world.ico`, `message_envelope_open.ico`, `directory_open_cabinet.ico`, `recycle_bin_empty.ico`, and `recycle_bin_full.ico`. Artwork by Microsoft. Deleted Items swaps between the empty and full bin.

- Altynai (april-hpxd/HILFER): desktop distortion, Mail echo and dead-pixel mechanics adapted from commit fe5c681a3478c3513d308213f20834de02de33e5. See docs/INTEGRATION.md.
